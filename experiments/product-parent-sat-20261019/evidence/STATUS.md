# SAT proof of the public parent's cached-product extension

19 October 2026, autonomous continuation. Research proof only; the frozen
parent4 candidate and its passing statistical gate remain unchanged.

Hypothesis: the previous bounded XOR/product matcher fails because it lacks
Boolean distributivity. A SAT miter over the exact 3,609-operation second
lease may prove product equality on arbitrary boundary wires, flags and
measurement outcomes without expanding enormous global expressions.

The interval is extracted from the exact PLAIN67 pre-tail body with SHA3-256
c143e90580bb0c6f6a12996a50147c179c4fdfe3a50d8af85350bf9e6d755e04.
Every raw interval and solver driver is hashed; SAT formula, result and proof
or countermodel are preserved. An arbitrary-cut countermodel is inconclusive
about reachable circuit states, never a circuit failure verdict.

Four proof and mutation tests run first. Solver Z3 5.1.0.0 is installed only
under this research directory. A 32 GiB address-space cap and one solver
thread apply. A successful local value identity still needs integration with
the global clean-donor, untouched-donor, effective-guard and phase checks
before any fast nonce filter is qualified. This experiment enables no filter,
nonce screening, census rerun, official benchmark or upload.
